About This Collection

Almost all historians, whatever their religious views, agree that Jesus of Nazareth existed. Most also accept that he was baptized by John the Baptist and crucified under the Roman prefect Pontius Pilate. This site gathers the main ancient evidence behind that agreement.

A note on scope: historical evidence can confirm people, places, and events. It cannot settle faith claims such as the resurrection or Jesus' divinity, so those questions are left to theology and personal conviction.
