Advertising Policy

Why we accept advertising

The Historical Jesus is a free educational website published by VSC Technology Services ("VSC", "we", "us"). Carefully chosen advertising helps cover the cost of research, hosting, and new content. Our first commitment is to our readers. The site exists to present the historical and archaeological evidence honestly, and no advertisement may compromise that.

Who may advertise

We welcome advertisers whose products or services fit the educational purpose of this site, including:

  • Christian universities, colleges, and seminaries
  • Bible schools, Bible courses, and Bible study programs
  • Study Bibles, Bible software, and Bible websites or apps
  • Christian and academic publishers, bookstores, and authors
  • Museums, exhibitions, and archaeology or history magazines
  • Holy Land, biblical archaeology, and educational tour operators
  • Churches, ministries, and Christian nonprofit organizations
  • Christian media, such as radio, podcasts, documentaries, and conferences
  • Other reputable businesses that, in our judgment, are a good fit for our readers

What we do not accept

We will not accept advertising for, or from businesses mainly involved in:

  • Political candidates, parties, campaigns, or political issue advocacy
  • Gambling, lotteries, or sweepstakes
  • Alcohol, tobacco, vaping, cannabis, or recreational drugs
  • Cryptocurrency, trading, "get rich quick" schemes, or high-interest lending
  • Health "cures," miracle products, or unproven medical or supplement claims
  • Adult content, dating, or relationship services
  • Weapons and ammunition
  • Occult or psychic services
  • Products or claims that contradict the evidence presented on this site, such as advertising an unverified relic or a claimed discovery of the Ark of the Covenant as fact
  • Content that attacks or demeans any faith, denomination, ethnic group, or person
  • Anything false, misleading, illegal, or that we believe would harm our readers or our reputation

We may decline any advertisement for any reason. We do not have to explain our decision.

How ads appear

  • Ads appear only in the Sponsors & Partners section. They are never placed inside the evidence entries, news, or videos.
  • Every ad is clearly labeled "Ad", "Sponsor", or "Affiliate link".
  • An ad consists of a logo or image, a name, a short description, and a link to the advertiser's website.
  • No pop-ups, auto-playing video or audio, animations, or tracking code. We host ad images ourselves, and advertisers may not add scripts, pixels, or cookies to our site.
  • Links to advertisers are marked as sponsored links for search engines, as search engine guidelines require.

Editorial independence

Advertisers and sponsors have no influence over the content of this site. Buying an ad does not buy coverage, favorable mention, a change to any entry or label, or removal of any content. If an advertiser's product or field is discussed on the site, it is treated the same as anything else.

Reader privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share our visitors' personal information with advertisers. We may share overall, anonymous statistics, such as monthly visitor counts, so advertisers can judge the value of an ad.

How to advertise

Use the "Advertise with us" form on the main page. Tell us about your organization and the ad you have in mind. We review every request and reply by email with availability and pricing.
